# Method core after V7 and V11

## What is no longer the method

Two ideas have now failed locked/preregistered tests and must not be silently restored:

1. **disagreement as an acquisition score** — fixed scalar disagreement allocation failed in V5;
2. **a fixed 50/10/40 dual-observer allocation as a generally superior deployment rule** — locked V7 failed;
3. **raw separate outputs + four high/low states as a generally transferable failure-localisation representation** — V11 claim D.

Therefore the method is not “combine PolliPi and InsePi and choose the best score,” and it is not “disagreement itself identifies the fault.”

## What remains supported independently

### Protected random exploration

The protected probability-sample component has analytical sampling guarantees and V9 design-based inference evidence. It remains the ecological denominator/reference design and the independent audit path for shared misses.

### Distinct observation questions

PolliPi and InsePi answer different questions:

- biological evidence: what looks like an ecological event candidate?
- observability risk: what observation-process conditions can produce false, missed or misattributed events?

Keeping those questions separate prevents one objective from defining the other's truth. This is a software/epistemic architecture, not a claim that their statistical errors are independent.

### Negative generations as evidence

V5, V7 and V11 are part of the method history. A failed generation is not replaced by tuning under the same label; it localises which assumption requires a new experimental generation.

## Narrowed contradiction-guided hypothesis

After V11, contradiction becomes useful only when its causal meaning is made identifiable by the experiment.

The working V12 hypothesis is therefore:

> Preserve purpose-distinct observer outputs, independently manipulate the causes each observer is intended to respond to, and use violations of preregistered causal response expectations to generate/localise fault hypotheses; retain protected random audit for states that both observers can miss.

This is **contradiction-guided experimental design**, not contradiction-score optimisation.

## Causal probing instead of quadrant classification

Let `Z_E` manipulate event presence and `Z_D` manipulate observation disturbance. Let `E` and `O` be the two observer outputs.

The primary diagnostic object is the factorial response matrix

```text
J = [ Δ_event E    Δ_disturbance E ]
    [ Δ_event O    Δ_disturbance O ]
```

and the two event×disturbance interactions.

A diagnostic contradiction is defined against a preregistered expected response, for example:

- `Z_E` changes but `E` does not respond while `O` remains stable;
- `Z_D` changes but `O` does not respond although a physical sensor/log confirms the intervention;
- both own-responses collapse only under a common optical manipulation;
- both observer outputs are low in an unaudited natural clip, which remains unresolved until protected audit supplies independent truth.

No causal label is inferred from `E-O` alone.

## Relation to latent disturbance inference

The observability programme is not target recognition. Its stronger future form infers latent exogenous disturbance from spatiotemporal visual signatures — shared motion, non-rigid coherence, photometric change, blur/texture loss, occlusion and related cues — then reports observation risk and uncertainty before ecological interpretation.

The physical V12 intervention logs and external sensors are evaluation truth only. They must not become InsePi inputs. This preserves the scientific question: can the visual stream itself reveal the effect of an otherwise unseen disturbance?

## Current evidence ceiling

At present it is defensible to claim:

- protected exploration is a valid sampling/inference safeguard under its stated design;
- fixed disagreement allocation and fixed 50/10/40 superiority have been falsified;
- the naive V11 contradiction-state localisation algorithm has been falsified;
- distinct observers plus frozen negative generations provide an auditable development scaffold.

It is **not yet** defensible to claim that contradiction-guided development is generally superior. That claim now depends on a causally identified, held-out physical V12 experiment.
