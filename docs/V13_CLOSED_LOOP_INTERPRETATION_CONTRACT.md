## Closed-loop target

The cross-repository target is not merely physical classification accuracy. It is the stronger observation-design sequence:

```text
current evidence
    |
    v
compatible physical failure classes
    |
    +--------------------------+
    |                          |
    v                          v
retained observer channels   candidate interventions
(V3-like refinement)         (MROD-like prospective refinement)
    |                          |
    +------------+-------------+
                 v
        finer distinctions
                 |
                 v
             Boundary
        remaining equivalence
```

V13 supplies one controlled physical testbed for this loop. It does **not** validate natural pollinator detection, ecological prevalence, or universal transfer.

## Predeclared compatible-world object

For V13, the physically declared latent class vocabulary is:

- `event_side`;

- `shared_optical`;
- `no_fault`.

Before diagnostic interventions, these are the relevant compatible physical failure worlds. The held-out task is to determine which class remains compatible with the same-stream observer responses while keeping held-out class truth sealed until prediction commitment.

The two retained observer channels are:

The candidate future observations are the paired physical interventions:

- `event_restore`;

- `shared_restore`.

## Mapping to V3

V3-level physical support requires evidence that retaining distinct event and observability response channels yields a stricter held-out distinction than deterministic poorer summaries.

The frozen V13 comparator set provides this without adding a new post-hoc analysis:

- `event_only`;
- `observability_only`;
- `early_scalar_fusion`;
- `dual_observer_vector`.

Safe interpretation:

- if `dual_observer_vector` materially outperforms the poorer retained representations under the frozen evaluator, V13 supports a **physical-domain strict-refinement instance**: retaining additional non-redundant observation information improved discrimination among the predeclared physical classes;
- if it merely ties the best comparator, the physical refinement claim is conditional/weak rather than strict;
- if it underperforms materially, V13 does not support a physical V3 strict-refinement claim.

This does not license named nuisance subtraction, natural-field transfer, or the claim that every richer representation is useful.

## Mapping to Boundary

For V13, the operational Boundary is the residual equivalence among the four predeclared physical classes under the currently revealed observer/intervention responses.

A Boundary refinement is supported when a newly revealed intervention response changes the admissible class set from a larger set to a proper subset for held-out blocks under the frozen development-fitted diagnostic model.

The strongest V13 report currently exposes prediction accuracy rather than an explicit per-prefix compatible-set ledger. Therefore:

- A/B/C/D may support **successful physical mechanism localisation**;
- they do not, by themselves, prove an exact rank reduction or quantify a formal identified-set dimension in the `boundary` repository sense.

Any future exact compatible-set/rank claim must be generated from a predeclared mapping, not reconstructed after seeing held-out truth.

## Mapping to MROD

V13 already contains a prospective-observation analogue: the generic diagnostic planner chooses an intervention order before the corresponding held-out treatment truth is revealed, with a primary budget of two interventions.

Safe interpretation:

- successful budget-2 held-out localisation supports the proposition that **sequentially selected physical interventions can resolve predeclared mechanism ambiguity under a frozen planner**;
- improvement from one intervention to two is evidence that the second acquired response adds useful conditional information;
- the full-battery result is a ceiling and must not be relabelled as the performance of the budget-2 selection policy;
- V13 does not compare the intervention order against a random-order physical baseline, so it does not independently reproduce the MROD G2 superiority-to-random result.

Thus V13 can supply physical transfer of the *prospective refinement mechanism*, but not a new universal optimality claim.

## A/B/C/D cross-repository interpretation

### A — `material_physical_distinct_channel_advantage`

If the frozen evaluator returns A, the following are recoverable:

1. **InsePi:** strong blinded physical transfer of distinct event/observability intervention signatures across new days and scenes;
2. **V3:** a controlled physical strict-refinement instance is supported because the dual retained representation exceeds the best frozen poorer comparator by at least the preregistered margin;
3. **MROD-like prospective branch:** the frozen two-intervention sequential diagnostic policy physically localises the predeclared failure class with high absolute performance and cluster floor;
4. **closed-loop synthesis:** retained information plus selected future intervention responses can jointly produce finer physical distinctions in one blinded observation system.

Still not licensed under A:

- natural pollinator detection accuracy;
- ecological prevalence or visit-rate validity;
- universal V3 reference benefit;
- universal MROD optimality;
- exact Boundary rank reduction unless separately predeclared and computed.

### B — `conditional_physical_causal_identification`

If the evaluator returns B:

- physical controlled intervention diagnosis is supported conditionally;
- dual-channel retention is at least as good as the best frozen comparator under the B rule;
- the loop has a positive physical instance, but the stronger "material distinct-channel advantage" is not recovered;
- wording must remain conditional and domain-specific.

### C — `mixed_physical_intervention_transfer`

If the evaluator returns C:

- the physical result is mixed;
- V12 synthetic identification does not cleanly transport to the V13 held-out physical domain;
- no unified physical-loop closure may be claimed;
- cluster/intervention-specific failures become the scientific result rather than grounds for retuning V13.

### D — `physical_intervention_identification_not_established`

If the evaluator returns D:

- physical identification is not established under the frozen V13 design;
- no physical V3 strict-refinement or closed-loop closure claim is licensed from V13;
- the failure must be retained as the transport boundary for the synthetic V12/V3 results;
- a new design, if pursued, must be a new generation rather than a repair of V13.
